# Caller guide

A short guide for callers and WhatsApp admins. Everything works on your phone.

## Signing in

1. Open the link the owner gave you and sign in with your email and the **temporary password**.
2. Choose your own password (at least 10 characters). Don't share it.

You only ever see the students assigned to you.

## Today

**Today** shows:

- your calls so far against your daily target;
- how many people you reached;
- callbacks due today, with **overdue ones in red**.

Tap **Start calling** to begin.

## Calling

The app shows one student at a time. It picks who's next in this order:

1. **Overdue callbacks**
2. **Callbacks due today**
3. Students **not called yet**
4. Students called longest ago

For each student:

1. Tap **Call**. Your phone's dialler opens.
2. After the call, tap what happened, e.g. **Answered**, **No answer**, **Interested**, **Callback requested**.
3. Add a note if it helps. Minutes are optional.
4. For **Callback requested**, pick a time: **In 2 hours**, **Tomorrow morning** (9 AM), **Tomorrow evening** (6 PM), or choose a date and time. All times are Sri Lanka time.
5. Tap **Save & next**.

Made a mistake? Tap **Undo** in the message at the top within 10 seconds. The student comes back with what you typed.

If your connection drops while saving, the app puts you back on that student with everything you typed still there. Just tap **Save** again.

Tap **Skip** to move on without saving anything.

**On a computer**, you can use the keyboard:

- `1`–`9` choose the outcome;
- `N` saves and moves to the next student;
- `C` calls;
- `W` opens WhatsApp.

## WhatsApp

1. Tap **WhatsApp** and pick a message (Sinhala or English). WhatsApp opens with the message filled in for that student.
2. **Check it and press send in WhatsApp.** The app never sends messages by itself.
3. Back in the app, tap **Yes, mark as sent**.

The **WhatsApp** page lists all your students. Tick **Not yet messaged** to see who still needs a message. The **Sent** tick box records (or un-records) that you messaged someone.

## Callbacks

The **Callbacks** page has tabs for **Overdue**, **Today**, **Upcoming** and **Done**. For each callback you can:

- **Call now**;
- **Reschedule** it;
- mark it **Done**;
- **Cancel** it (you'll be asked for a reason).

Calling a student back from calling mode completes their callback automatically.

## My contacts

Search by name, school or phone number, and filter by stage. Tap a student to see or edit their details and their full history of calls, messages and callbacks.

If a new student calls you directly, use **Add contact**. They're added to your list.

**Enrolled** means their payment has been received. The app asks you to confirm before marking someone enrolled.

## Privacy

- Students' phone numbers are private. Don't copy them into other groups or apps.
- If someone asks not to be contacted, set their stage to **Do not contact**.
